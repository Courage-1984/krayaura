This illustrated artwork represents my connection to the future planet upon which we live. It portrays a futuristic metropolis that floats in space using rocket power to float in space. In this city, there are flying vehicles, which reflect future technological and transportation improvements. It also uses cool colours like blue to give a future world mood.

At the bottom of the poster, there are unused roads, which symbolize the changes that have taken place over time. As we evolve and embrace new technologies, the old ways become outdated and fall out of use.

This artwork illustrates the idea that the future is continuously changing and evolving and that it is up to us to adapt and welcome these changes. It motivates us to look forward to the future with hope and enthusiasm, knowing that we can change it and make the world a better place than it has ever been. My connection to the future is the contribution I can make to the world's growth through the stories and concepts I tell or produce through the artwork I create.
